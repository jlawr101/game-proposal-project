Siegfried, The Blood-Drenched Bulwark - A dragon slayer with high defense due to bathing in dragon’s blood.  Part of the Deviant trio(Heretic)

Dragon-Scale Hazards: The Battle Box walls actively shrink and grow like a crushing trash compactor, representing his suffocating defensive presence.

The Shield Charge: Siegfried charges horizontally across the box. He doesn't just hurt you; his shield wipes out the platforms you need to stand on, forcing you to manage your Stamina Bar perfectly to stay airborne using precise wall-jumps.Exposing the Flaw:

To win, you must survive the gauntlet until the 25% Break, use a dialogue option or a puzzle item to distract him, and forcefully swap your gear to a precise piercing tool (like an excavator pick) to strike his singular weak spot.

For the lore: In a world of floating islands, "dragons" aren't standard mythical beasts—they are massive, terrifying leviathans that fly through the bottomless void between islands. Siegfried hunted them down alone. By bathing in their black, corrosive blood, his skin hardened into an impenetrable, metallic hide. He doesn't care about the King or the hierarchy; he respects only raw survival and strength.( I chose this for we can have a third party bosses/ side bosses after the final boss),(This also allows us to make a second game where we can go to the realm they come from by riding on one allowing a continuation of the series.)

Zephyr, The Sky-Stitched Weaver

Where Siegfried is a heavy, immovable mountain of blood-hardened armor, Zephyr is the terrifying opposite: a relentless, razor-sharp gale. He represents the unrelenting wind and vertical instability of the world of floating islands. Part of the Deviant trio(Heretic)

The Lore

While Siegfried hunted the Void-Leviathans for raw survival, Zephyr hunted them to conquer the skies. He was the first scout to map the deep trenches between islands. During a disastrous hunt, his lower body was severed by a Leviathan's tail. To survive, he used ancient, forbidden weaving magic to "stitch" his remaining flesh directly to the living, translucent wings and nervous system of a juvenile Void-Harpy.

He doesn't care about the King or the hierarchy because he views land-dwellers as flightless cattle. He lives in a perpetual state of agonizing, wind-whipped euphoria, guarding the high-altitude jet streams that lead to the Leviathan breeding grounds.

(Torture)The Battle Box Hazard: The Vortex Core The Battle Box walls don't crush you—they spin and violently tilt.The Mechanic: The entire Battle Box rotates 45 to 90 degrees at unpredictable intervals. What was once a safe floor suddenly becomes a vertical wall.The Hazard: A localized hurricane sits at the bottom of the box. If the box tilts and you slip off your platform, you fall into the vortex, taking rapid-fire wind-slashing damage until you can wall-jump your way back up.

The Signature Move: The Gale Ribbon: Zephyr flies in looping, chaotic infinity-patterns across the screen.The Attack: As he flies, he trails glowing, razor-sharp "sky-threads" behind him. These threads permanently cut the Battle Box into smaller, geometric zones.The Danger: Passing through a thread chunks your health and inflicts a "Bleed" status. Because the box is constantly tilting and rotating, you have to remember where the invisible, vertical threads are so you don't fall sideways into them when gravity shifts.

Exposing the Flaw: The 25% Break Zephyr moves too fast to hit normally; your attacks simply graze his feathers. To defeat him, you must endure the rotating chaos until the 25% Break.

The Trigger: You must use a heavy, weighted item (like a Magnetic Grapple or a Grounded Anchor) to latch his sky-threads directly to the rotating walls of the Battle Box.The Strike: When the box shifts again, his own threads pull taut, snapping his wings back and pinning him motionless against the wall. You must rapidly switch to a slashing weapon (like a crescent scythe) to sever the magical stitches holding his torso to his avian half.

The Drop: The Slipstream Chains: Chains that you use to control the leviathan to get to the next game.

Morphaea, The Void-Wept Alchemist Where Siegfried is the unyielding earth and Zephyr is the wild wind, Morphaea represents the corrosive depths. She is an outcast scholar who didn't hunt the Void-Leviathans for glory or survival; she hunted them for their black, volatile ink, using it to rewrite the laws of matter. Part of the Deviant trio(Heretic)

The Lore: Morphaea was once the Chief Arcanist of the Floating Islands. She realized the islands were slowly losing their buoyancy and would eventually plunge into the void. When the King refused to fund her research, she went into the deep trenches alone. She discovered that Leviathan ink dissolves reality itself, allowing them to swim through the vacuum of space.To withstand its toxicity, she replaced her own blood and nervous system with a fluid network of alchemical glass piping. She is cold, detached, and brilliant. She views the surface world as a dying house and herself as the architect of the escape hatch. She guards the Abyssal Forge, where the portal to the sequel’s realm is located.

The Battle Box Hazard: The Ink PhasingThe Battle Box walls don't move, but the platforms themselves become unstable.The Mechanic: Morphaea floods the bottom 25% of the Battle Box with a rising tide of Corrosive Void Ink.

The Hazard: Periodically, she flashes a color code across the screen. Instantly, all solid platforms of that color dissolve into liquid ink. If you are standing on one, you plunge straight down into the acidic floor. You must constantly track the color cycles and time your jumps to land only on the "solid" reality platforms.

The Signature Move: The Alchemical Cascade: Morphaea doesn't engage in melee combat; she commands the arena like a deadly laboratory.The Attack: She smashes giant, volatile flasks against the ceiling. Droplets of burning acid rain down across the entire box in a bullet-hell pattern.The Danger: Where the acid hits a platform, it creates a permanent "burn hole." This restricts your safe standing zones even further, forcing you to use incredibly precise, tight movements to avoid both the falling droplets and the dissolving floors.

 Exposing the Flaw: The 25% BreakMorphaea is protected by an impenetrable, shifting barrier of liquid ink that dissolves any physical weapon or projectile on contact.

The Trigger: At the 25% Break, her alchemical glass core overheats, forcing her to siphon ink directly from the arena floor to cool down. You must use a specific puzzle item(Void Flower) and drop it into the rising ink tide.The Strike: The flower instantly flash-freezes the ink grid, travelling up her siphoning tubes and locking her glass piping in brittle ice. You must instantly switch to a heavy blunt weapon (like a warhammer) to shatter her external glass shell and expose her vulnerable, organic core.

The Drop: The Catalyst Flask: Used to open the portal to the next realm.
